Who We Are

Built at the Intersection of Mining, Technology & Global Capital

BNB Mining & Zain Co. International is a Miami-headquartered enterprise focused on Bitcoin mining operations, cryptocurrency infrastructure deployment, proprietary mining software development, and international investment research.

Founded by Suraj Kumar Jha and Zain — two brothers whose complementary expertise bridges India's technology sector and the global digital asset landscape — the company is structured for international scale while maintaining precision in its core operations.
